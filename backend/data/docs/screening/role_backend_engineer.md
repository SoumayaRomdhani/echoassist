# Open Role — Senior Backend Engineer (Voice & AI Platform)

We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to work on the voice and AI platform that
powers ACTEEK agents. This is a full-time, permanent position.

## Responsibilities
- Design and build asynchronous Python services (FastAPI, asyncio) that serve the
  voice pipeline and the dashboard API.
- Work on retrieval-augmented generation: chunking, embeddings, vector search,
  and grounding quality.
- Integrate speech-to-text, large language models, and text-to-speech behind
  provider-agnostic interfaces.
- Own reliability: observability, latency budgets, and graceful degradation.

## Requirements
- At least 5 years of professional backend experience, mostly in Python.
- Strong experience with FastAPI or a similar async framework, and PostgreSQL.
- Comfort with Docker and cloud deployment.
- Working proficiency in English; French is a plus.

## Nice to have
- Experience with vector databases (Qdrant, Pinecone) and LLM tooling.
- Exposure to real-time audio or telephony systems.

The role reports to the Voice Platform squad lead.
