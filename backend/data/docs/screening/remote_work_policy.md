# Remote Work & Location Policy

ACTEEK is remote-first. The Senior Backend Engineer role is fully remote within
the EU and Tunisia. You may also work from the Lyon office if you prefer.

## Working hours and overlap
There are no fixed office hours, but we ask for at least four hours of daily
overlap with the 10:00–18:00 Central European Time window so squads can
collaborate. Outside that overlap, you manage your own schedule.

## Meetings
We default to asynchronous communication. Squads have one short daily sync and one
weekly planning meeting; everything else is written.

## Travel
The whole company meets in person twice a year for a week-long onsite, with travel
and accommodation covered. Occasional travel for these gatherings is expected, but
day-to-day work never requires commuting.

## Equipment
Remote employees receive a laptop and a home-office stipend. Reliable internet is
required; ACTEEK can contribute to the cost.
