# Hiring & Interview Process

Our process for the Senior Backend Engineer role has four stages and typically
takes two to three weeks from first contact to offer.

1. **Phone screening (20 minutes).** An automated AI screening assistant confirms
   your background, availability, and salary expectations. With your consent, the
   call is recorded for quality.
2. **Technical interview (60 minutes).** A live conversation about your past work,
   plus a system-design discussion. No live coding puzzle.
3. **Take-home review (90 minutes, optional).** A small, focused task you can do on
   your own schedule; we review it together in the next call.
4. **Final conversation (45 minutes).** A discussion with the squad lead about ways
   of working, expectations, and your questions.

We aim to give a decision within three business days of the final conversation.
Candidates always speak with a human before any offer is made. Reasonable
accommodations are available at any stage on request.
