# ACTEEK — Company Overview

ACTEEK is a software company that builds an AI Agent Marketplace, where teams
deploy specialized assistants such as the CRM Agent, the Legal Agent, and the
PDF Agent. The company was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Lyon, France,
with a fully distributed engineering team across Europe and North Africa.

Our mission is to make autonomous, reliable AI agents accessible to non-technical
teams. We value pragmatic engineering, measurable quality, and strong data
privacy. ACTEEK is GDPR-compliant and stores customer data within the EU.

The engineering organization is about 60 people, split into small product squads.
Each squad owns one agent end to end, from the model pipeline to the dashboard.
The company language is English, but most customer-facing work is in French and
English.
