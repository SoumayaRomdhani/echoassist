# Compensation & Benefits

## Salary
The Senior Backend Engineer role has a base salary range of 65,000 to 85,000 euros
per year, depending on experience and location. The range is reviewed annually.
Offers near the top of the range require strong, directly relevant experience.

## Equity
Permanent employees receive stock options that vest over four years, with a
one-year cliff.

## Benefits
- 28 days of paid annual leave, plus public holidays.
- Private health insurance for the employee and dependents.
- A yearly learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, books, and conferences.
- Home-office stipend and a hardware budget for your setup.
- Parental leave above the legal minimum.

## Bonus
There is a discretionary annual performance bonus of up to 10% of base salary,
tied to individual and company objectives.
